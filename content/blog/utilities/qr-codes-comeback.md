# Why QR Codes are Making a Huge Comeback

Once thought of as a passing fad, QR codes have cemented themselves as a crucial bridge between the physical and digital worlds. 

## The Catalyst
The need for touchless menus and easy app downloads during recent years forced everyone to learn how to scan a QR code with their native camera app.
